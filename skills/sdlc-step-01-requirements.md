# Requirements Analysis

Read `user-story.md` (or ask for the raw story if missing), extract the problem statement, stakeholders, functional requirements, non-functional requirements, scope, assumptions, and open questions, then write `requirements.md` and summarize the outcome.

## Guardrails
- Follow `CLAUDE.md` and the repo conventions.
- Do not assume approval without explicit user confirmation.
- Keep the work scoped to the current SDLC phase and the relevant artifacts.
- Produce a concise summary at the end.
